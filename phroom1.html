How should we measure your space?

We measure the space in your photo and check it against the machine.

Why not use my hand, or just the photo?

Hands differ in size by 15% or more from person to person, and a hand held up to the camera is much nearer than the floor being measured — a millimetre's slip there becomes centimetres at the machine's spot. A sheet of A4 paper is 21 × 29.7 cm everywhere, and it lies exactly where the machine will stand.

The photo alone can only guess the room's scale from things of typical size (doors, counters, ceiling height) — good for seeing how it looks, but it can be 10% out. That's fine for a big space, not for a tight one.
